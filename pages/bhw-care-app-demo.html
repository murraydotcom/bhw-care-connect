Why both, and what to watch

Progesterone protects the lining of the uterus while you are on estrogen. If continuous dosing causes troublesome bleeding, 200 mg nightly for 12–14 nights a month is an alternative. Some progesterone capsules contain peanut oil and can cause real drowsiness and dizziness — it adds to hydroxyzine, cyclobenzaprine and cannabis. Transdermal estrogen appears to carry less clotting risk than tablets, though not zero.

Before continuing past a bridgeConfirm uterine/endometrial status and any unexplained bleeding; breast pathology and current mammography; no prior VTE/PE, stroke, MI, active liver disease or pregnancy. Cervical surveillance continues on the usual schedule.

Two SSRIs together raise the serotonergic and sedating load, and starting hormones is the right moment to sort out whether this is a cross-taper or intended long term. The reasoning
Why fiber and food come before probiotics

PHGG is a soluble fiber that feeds the bacteria already in your gut and helps regularity. Evidence for off-the-shelf probiotics is inconsistent and strain-specific, so a high-count blend is not the place to start. Aim for 25–30 g fiber a day and 20–30 different plants a week, built up gradually.

Why this is not called "boosting IgA"

Your total IgA is low-normal, not deficient. Blood IgA subclasses do not measure the secretory IgA in your gut, and no supplement has been shown to normalize that pathway. So the goal is a well-fed mucosal barrier, not a number.

Calcium, and what to skip

1,000–1,200 mg of calcium a day, food first. Do not cut foods based on food-IgG panels — they do not show allergy or intolerance and they narrow your nutrition for no gain.
