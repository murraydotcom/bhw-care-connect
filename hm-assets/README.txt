BHW dashboards — adding your real body / brain / hologram images
================================================================

The pages already look for your licensed images by name. To use them,
just copy your files into the  hm-assets/  folder and rename them exactly.
If a file is missing, the page quietly falls back to a built-in drawn
placeholder — nothing breaks.


1) LIGHT figures (Overview + program re-skins)
----------------------------------------------
    hm-assets/body-female.png    ← female figure (Overview, program pages)
    hm-assets/body-male.png      ← male figure (kept ready for male patients)
    hm-assets/brain-neuro.png    ← glowing brain (CharmEd page)

  PNG with a TRANSPARENT background looks best here — the figure floats on
  the light glassy stage. A white background will show as a white block.


2) HOLOGRAM heroes (dark "scan viewport" pages)
-----------------------------------------------
  These are the futuristic dark holographic body images (the ones on the
  black/space background with the HUD rings + organ call-outs already baked
  in). They fill a dark scan-viewport, so the dark background is CORRECT here
  — keep it, do NOT cut it out.

    hm-assets/cardio-hero.png    ← teal body + red heart & vasculature  (Cardiovascular)
    hm-assets/gi-hero.png        ← GI / liver / microbiome map           (Digestive — optional)
    hm-assets/neuro-hero.png     ← central nervous system / neurons      (Neurological — optional)
    hm-assets/immune-hero.png    ← lymphatic / red-zone activation        (Immune — optional)
    hm-assets/vagus-hero.png     ← vagus nerve / gut-brain axis           (spare / gut-brain)

  Right now only the Cardiovascular page is wired to its hologram hero
  (cardio-hero.png). Drop that file in and reopen bhw-cardio.html — the
  holographic heart body appears in the scan viewport automatically.
  Say the word and I'll wire the other hologram heroes into GI / Neuro /
  Immune the same way.


Tips
----
- Keep file names lowercase and exactly as above.
- Square-ish images (about 1:1) fit the hologram viewport best.
- If a heart glow or organ tag sits a little off once your real image loads,
  tell me and I'll nudge it to match the figure.
